How it works

Visiting Dublin and don't want to lose a week of training? Three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick how you want to move

    Boxing, jiu-jitsu, yoga, climbing, a sauna after a long flight. Browse by discipline or by area.

  2. 02

    Check the price and the rules

    Every listing shows the drop-in price and whether you need to book, so there are no surprises at the door.

  3. 03

    Turn up and train

    Book or pay the studio directly. Movelty is free and never takes a cut.
